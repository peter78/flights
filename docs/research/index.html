AI Flight Explorer

Research and design notes for a natural-language flight search tool with complex multi-leg, multi-airport, intermodal, and total-cost-transparent itineraries.

The problem in one paragraph

Traditional flight search treats a trip as a single origin airport, a single destination airport, and two dates. Real travel rarely fits that shape. A user near Munich might be willing to take a train up to four hours to reach a cheaper airport, fly into one city, and return from another. They want to compare options across many combinations of departure airports, arrival airports, ground transit, dates, and airlines — ranked by total cost including bag fees, parking, and ground tickets — expressed in natural language.

What's in scope

  • Natural-language input — "fly to Crete next month, willing to train up to 4h, return to anywhere in southern Germany"
  • Open-jaw trips — arrival city ≠ departure city
  • Multi-origin fan-out — consider multiple departure airports near the user
  • Intermodal — train or bus to a cheaper airport, then fly
  • Date-flexible — ±N days, cheapest week
  • Total-cost transparency — surface bag fees, seat selection, parking, and ground tickets up front
  • Protected intermodal — detect rail-as-flight programs (Lufthansa Express Rail, AF TGVAir, etc.) and surface them prominently

Key decisions

Form factor
Web app and mobile (PWA-first; React Native/Expo later)
Flight data source
fast-flights (Google Flights scraper) for v1, with a swappable FlightProvider interface so Duffel/Amadeus/Kiwi can drop in later
Fee data
LLM-extracted from each airline's published fees page, cached weekly in Postgres
NLU
Claude Haiku for live parsing/disambiguation; open-source models (Llama 3.3 / DeepSeek V3) for the batch fee-extraction worker
Ground scope
US + EU, all modes (drive / transit / rail / bus)
Ground pricing
Real prices where APIs allow (DB HAFAS, FlixBus, Google Directions); estimates from Rome2Rio elsewhere
Protected intermodal
v1 feature: detect and deep-link to airline-bookable combined products
Booking
Out of scope for v1 — deep-link to airline / Google Flights
